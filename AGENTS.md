# Agent Instructions

These instructions apply to AI agents working on this repository.
Follow [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) like every contributor; the rules below are specific to agents.

## Working With Humans

- When in doubt, favor clarifying with the human you are working for over guessing: unclear or ambiguous requests, conflicting rules or conventions, or choices between several reasonable options.
- Before proposing how to solve a problem, find why it occurs: state its cause, and prefer the options that remove it over those that only treat a symptom.
- When you propose design options, or analyze one for the human you are working for, give the pros and cons of each: every non-trivial design choice is a trade-off.

## Pull Requests

- Merge a pull request only when the human you are working for explicitly asks you to, never on your own initiative.

## Commits

- Sign off every commit (`git commit -s`) with the identity of the human you are working for, who certifies the contribution by reviewing it before merging, and identify yourself with a `Co-Authored-By` trailer.
